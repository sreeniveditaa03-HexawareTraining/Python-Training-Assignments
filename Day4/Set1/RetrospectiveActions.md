## 2. What Went Well

**Effective Sprint Planning:** Historical velocity helped the Developers select a manageable 23-point sprint.

**Team Collaboration:** Daily Scrum updates helped identify and coordinate the resolution of integration blockers.

**Adaptability:** The Product Owner and Developers collaboratively adjusted the sprint scope after the refund request.

**Quality Focus:** Completed stories were checked against acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done.

## 3. What Could Be Improved

**Dependency Identification:** Payment gateway configuration and test data requirements should be checked earlier.

**Requirement Clarity:** Refund eligibility and duplicate-processing rules required additional clarification.

**Uneven Work Completion:** The refund story was completed on the final day, increasing delivery risk.

**Story Slicing:** Large stories should be divided into smaller, independently testable increments where possible.
