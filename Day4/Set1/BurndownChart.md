## 1. Objective

The Sprint Burndown Chart tracks remaining work throughout the sprint and helps the Scrum Team identify delays, progress trends, and delivery risks.

## 2. Burndown Calculation

## 5. Trend Interpretation

**Days 1–2 – Initial Progress:** The actual burndown remains flat at 23 SP because no story has fully satisfied the Definition of Done.

**Day 3 – First Completion:** Authentication (8 SP) is completed, reducing remaining work to 15 SP.

**Day 4 – Mid-Sprint Change:** DWA-103 (8 SP) is removed and DWA-119 (8 SP) is added. The net scope change is zero, so remaining work stays at 15 SP.

**Days 5–6 – Progress:** Wallet Balance (5 SP) is completed on Day 6, reducing remaining work to 10 SP.

**Days 7–9 – Delivery Risk:** Progress is uneven. Transaction History (2 SP) is completed on Day 8, but the refund story remains unfinished through Day 9.

**Day 10 – Sprint Completion:** Refund to Wallet (8 SP) meets the Definition of Done, reducing remaining work to zero.
