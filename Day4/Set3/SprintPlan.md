## 4. Sprint Goal

By the end of the 10-day sprint, deliver a tested e-learning workflow that enables learners to enroll in courses, watch video lessons, complete quizzes, and track their learning progress, with all goal-critical stories satisfying the Definition of Done.

Course rating and feedback is additional sprint work, but it is not essential to achieving the Sprint Goal.

## 5. User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

### ELP-101 – Course Enrollment (8 SP)

- Learners can enroll in available courses.
- Duplicate enrollment is prevented.
- Enrolled courses appear on the learner dashboard.

### ELP-102 – Video Lesson Player (8 SP)

- Enrolled learners can access course videos.
- Play, pause, and resume controls work correctly.
- Video playback errors display meaningful messages.

### ELP-103 – Quiz and Assessment (5 SP)

- Learners can submit quiz answers.
- Quiz scores are calculated correctly.
- Results are saved against the learner's course.

### ELP-104 – Learning Progress Tracking (5 SP)

- Completed lessons update learner progress.
- Progress percentage is calculated correctly.
- Progress persists after logout and login.

### ELP-105 – Course Rating and Feedback (4 SP)

- Eligible learners can submit course ratings.
- Ratings are validated within the permitted range.
- Submitted feedback is saved and displayed correctly.

## 6. Initial Sprint Planning Decision

The team commits to 30 story points based on historical velocity and the selected backlog.

The Sprint Goal prioritizes enrollment, video learning, assessments, and progress tracking.

Non-goal-critical work may be renegotiated if unexpected business requirements arise.
