# Sprint Velocity Calculation – E-Learning Platform

**Project:** E-Learning Platform  
**Sprint:** Sprint 01 – Learning Experience  
**Sprint Duration:** 10 Working Days  
**Planned Velocity:** 30 Story Points  
**Sprint Status:** Closed 

## 1. Objective

Sprint velocity measures the total story points of Product Backlog items completed during a sprint according to the Definition of Done.

Partially completed stories are not counted toward completed velocity.

## 2. Final Sprint Scope

The proposed Day 6 certificate request was deferred because its API contract remained unresolved and the Developers could not confidently commit to completing it.

The original sprint scope therefore remains 30 SP.

## 3. Completed Story Points

| Jira Key | User Story | Estimated SP | Completion | Points Counted |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| ELP-101 | Course Enrollment | 8 | 100% – Done | 8 |
| ELP-102 | Video Lesson Player | 8 | 100% – Done | 8 |
| ELP-103 | Quiz and Assessment | 5 | 100% – Done | 5 |
| ELP-104 | Learning Progress Tracking | 5 | 100% – Done | 5 |
| ELP-105 | Course Rating and Feedback | 4 | 80% – In Progress | 0 |
| **Total** | | **30** | | **26** |

## 4. Velocity Calculation

**Sprint Velocity = Sum of Story Points for Done Stories**

= 8 + 8 + 5 + 5 + 0

**Actual Sprint Velocity = 26 Story Points**

### Completion Rate

Completion Rate = (Completed Story Points / Committed Story Points) × 100

= (26 / 30) × 100

**Sprint Completion Rate = 86.67%**

## 5. Treatment of Partially Completed Work

ELP-105 is estimated at 4 story points and is approximately 80% implemented.

Although 80% of 4 SP equals 3.2 SP mathematically, **the team does not count 3.2 SP toward velocity**.

Scrum velocity counts only work that satisfies the Definition of Done.

| Story Condition | Velocity Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Story fully Done | Full estimated story points |
| Story 80% complete | 0 SP |
| Story not started | 0 SP |
| Story blocked and incomplete | 0 SP |

Therefore, ELP-105 contributes **0 SP** to the completed velocity.

The unfinished story returns to the Product Backlog for reassessment and future planning. Its remaining work may be re-estimated or split as appropriate.

## 6. Sprint Outcome

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Planned Story Points | 30 SP |
| Completed Story Points | 26 SP |
| Incomplete Story Points | 4 SP |
| Actual Velocity | 26 SP |
| Completion Rate | 86.67% |
| Certificate Request | Deferred to Product Backlog |

The four goal-critical stories are complete, so the original Sprint Goal is achieved.

However, the team completes fewer points than its initial 30-point commitment.

## 7. Conclusion

The actual velocity is **26 story points**, not 29.2 points.

Partially completed work contributes zero points until the entire story meets the Definition of Done.

This ensures velocity reflects completed, usable work rather than implementation progress.
